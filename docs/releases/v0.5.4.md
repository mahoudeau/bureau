Sign in instead of holding a token, a guide that connects your agents, and Discord goes. This release also carries 0.5.0 to 0.5.3, which were never tagged on their own.

- **Sign in with OMAKO**, or any OpenID issuer. People get a role: owner, approver or viewer. The boss token stays as the way in when the issuer is down.
- **Each agent signs in at its own address**, `/mcp/agent/<name>`, and a new app waits on the Desk until an owner allows it.
- **`bureau login`** signs a terminal in as an agent and keeps the token in the Keychain.
- **A setup guide** at `/setup` connects consul and sol one step at a time, and checks each step worked before the next. It works with any AI app that can add a remote MCP server, not only Claude, and every other agent gets the same guide.
- **Scheduled tasks** do over MCP what they used to do with curl and a shared token.
- **Export and import** move a hub to another server, brain history included, secrets left out.
- **Discord, the review links and the view links are gone.** The Desk is where you rule now.

Coming from 0.4.0, read UPGRADING.md, "0.5.0: sign-in, and Discord goes". The first boot migrates `state.json` to schema 2, and 0.4.0 can't read it afterwards. Nothing else changes until you turn sign-in on.

Docker: `ghcr.io/mahoudeau/bureau:0.5.4`. Everything that changed is in [CHANGELOG.md](https://github.com/mahoudeau/bureau/blob/v0.5.4/CHANGELOG.md).
