See how the office is doing. A new Insights page, the same numbers for your agents, and a look you pick yourself.

- **Insights:** missions filed and closed per day, week or month, time from claim to done, how long work waits in your review, and your rulings, per project and per agent, with brain commits beside them. A calendar opens any day, hour by hour.
- **This week** on the Desk: done, filed and waiting since Monday.
- **`GET /api/stats` and the MCP tool `read_stats`**, so a weekly digest is one call. Each credential sees only its own projects.
- **Your look,** in Settings, Appearance: Paper or Ink, Marker or Pencil, one of five colours, and the ground behind the sheets. The office follows the colour.

Nothing to change by hand.

Docker: `ghcr.io/mahoudeau/bureau:0.5.6`. Everything that changed is in [CHANGELOG.md](https://github.com/mahoudeau/bureau/blob/v0.5.6/CHANGELOG.md).
