Your hub now tells you when a new release is out, and what to do before upgrading.

- **A line on the Desk when a release is out.** Settings, Updates shows the upgrade notes for every release between yours and the new one. Only the operator sees it. The check is two plain requests to GitHub, carrying nothing about your hub. `BUREAU_UPDATE_CHECK=off` stops it.
- **The setup guide opens on its welcome screen.** On a brand-new hub it used to skip it and open on "Make it yours".

Nothing to change on a running hub: pull and restart. More in UPGRADING.md, "0.5.8: release notices, and the guide's welcome screen".

Docker: `ghcr.io/mahoudeau/bureau:0.5.8`. Everything that changed is in [CHANGELOG.md](https://github.com/mahoudeau/bureau/blob/v0.5.8/CHANGELOG.md).
