About this composition

Back wall (2 tiled rows of wall-cracked.png) meets the floor (1 tiled row of floor-wood.png) at a single clean seam. The wall-monitor is mounted on the wall at a believable height, clear of the desk. The desk unit is typing-0.png — stage 1's self-contained agent+chair+desk+CRT sprite — grounded with its bottom edge exactly on the seam so nothing floats.

Per t-141's MANIFEST.md: the standalone desk-tidy.png/chair.png/crt-monitor.png sprites are deliberately NOT composited here — the typing frames already ship the full seated-at-desk-with-monitor illustration as one unit, and layering the standalone pieces underneath would duplicate (and likely misalign) furniture that's already inside that sprite.

  • World: 160×135 logical px, integer-scaled below.
  • Seam (wall/floor line): y=90.
  • All sprite scale traces to the shared 48px agent anchor (t-141 MANIFEST.md) — no rescaling applied here.
