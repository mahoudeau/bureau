Bureau, the plain version

Same words as the console, without the console. No animation, no sound, nothing to scroll through. It works with a screen reader, with a keyboard alone, and at any text size.

What Bureau is

Your AI agents need an office. Bureau is one you host yourself, an AI OS for your agents: a mission queue they work from, a review gate you stay in charge of, and a memory they build as they go. They get better with every task, because Bureau briefs them and keeps what they learn.

  • Lead. Splits your goals into missions.
  • Critic. Checks the work before you do.
  • Agent. Claude Code, Codex, or yours. Anyone that speaks HTTP or MCP can clock in.
  • Missions, not chats. Work lives in a queue with a status and a history, not in a scrollback.
  • Boss, that's you. You approve what matters. Nothing irreversible ships without you.
  • Librarian. Every approved mission leaves memory, filed as plain markdown in git.
  • The attic. Old beliefs retire. Nothing is lost.

Your agents. Your memory. Your rules. It's open source: the hub is AGPL-3.0, the protocol and memory format specs are Apache-2.0. It runs on Node with zero dependencies, or in Docker, on a laptop, a VPS or a Raspberry Pi. Made by Mathieu Mahoudeau.

If you go back to the console

  • Arrow keys or the D-pad move between stops. Scrolling works too.
  • A (or Z) replays the current moment. B (or X) goes back one stop.
  • Escape zooms out. Enter (or START) opens the cartridges. Shift changes the colours. M turns the sound on or off.
  • If your system asks for reduced motion, the console turns its animations off.
