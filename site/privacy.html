Privacy

Last updated 30 September 2026.

The waitlist

When you save your spot, I keep your email, your three answers (pro or personal, your role, self-host or hosted) and the date. That's it.

I use it for one thing: emailing you when the hosted Bureau opens. The answers help me decide what to build first. I don't sell it, share it or add you to anything else.

It's stored on a server in France (alwaysdata), and in Brevo, an email service based in France, which sends that one email. I keep it until the hosted version launches, plus twelve months, or until you ask me to delete it.

Starring on GitHub from the site

If you use the star button and approve it on GitHub, the site gets a token that can only star repositories. It stars Bureau once and the token is thrown away. Nothing about your GitHub account is kept.

The MCP server

The public MCP server at /mcp answers questions about Bureau from the text of this site. It needs no account, keeps no record of what you ask, and stores nothing. Like every web request, it passes through Cloudflare and the host, whose logs hold your IP address for a short time.

Everything else

No cookies, no trackers, no analytics script. The server counts visits on its own: how many times each page is read per day, the site that sent you, your country (as Cloudflare reports it) and whether it's a phone or a computer. It keeps only those daily totals, nothing about you. To count visitors once a day it uses a code made from your IP address and browser that changes every day and is never saved. Your browser remembers the palette you picked and whether sound is on. That stays on your device.

Deleting your data

Email mahoudeau@outlook.com from the address you signed up with and I'll delete it.
